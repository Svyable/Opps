Checked 2026-11-04. Internal decision record, not an application or a claim of eligibility.

Official ITU organizer: https://aiforgood.itu.int/about-us/innovation-factory/

## Svyable asset match and verification gates
Potentially relevant: Svyable/human-kind (human-centered AI), Control Evidence agent audit/evaluation and agent-business. These are candidate technical artifacts, **not** proof of registered startup status, product-market fit, impact users or revenue.
Before any application:
1. Verify legal entity registration, six-month operating history and total funding; secondary eligibility descriptions suggest these requirements, but **official application form terms were not inspected**.
2. Verify a live, demonstrable AI-core MVP, dated independent users and a specific measurable social/development outcome; do not present synthetic agent fixtures as external traction.
3. Verify source ownership, coauthor permissions, public-demo disclosure rights, and whether the team can pitch live.
4. Obtain official application and prize rules, geography, travel funding and IP/publicity consent terms; do not enter or accept them autonomously.
5. Check that a global monthly round is actually accepting applications, not merely scheduled.

## Conservative marginal EV and stop-loss
Prize-only illustrative model: $20,000 × 30% qualification feasibility × 1% chance of ultimately winning conditional on qualification − 3h × $35 applicant/pitch preflight = **−$45**. A realistic Geneva trip, live pitch, product adaptation and delivery would make this worse unless sponsored. The 1% conditional win rate is subjective, not derived from the organizer's finalist count. Mentorship, partnerships and licensing excluded because unpriced. The $20k is winner-only and cannot be treated as contracted revenue.
Reuse is moderate/high if a verified live human-kind/Control Evidence MVP exists; incremental work 3h preflight plus substantial unpriced pitch/travel if selected. With $105 effort, break-even combined probability is 0.525%, versus illustrative 0.3%; qualification evidence is absent.
**Decision: WATCH**, not PURSUE. No more autonomous pitch writing until an official global round and real product/entity/user evidence are established. No application, outreach, spend or contract.
