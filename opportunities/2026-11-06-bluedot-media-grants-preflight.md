# BlueDot Media Grants — 2026-11-06 eligibility and commercial preflight

**Decision: WATCH; newly discovered, not submitted.** Official source: https://bluedot.org/grants/media (checked 2026-11-06). This is a different program from BlueDot Rapid Grants and Career Transition Grants; do not merge their records.

## Why this is an existing-asset opportunity
Sven Hardy Benson's Shelf publicly documents **56 released books** and a working reading interface: https://github.com/Svyable/shelf/blob/main/README.md and https://svyable.github.io/shelf/reader/. Existing Desk research and books about AI, institutions, and human agency can seed a genuinely new, evidence-led AI-risk media series. Existing cinematic YouTube channels establish production experimentation, **not** a verified AI-risk audience. No audience size, sales, views, subscriber count, conversion or publication reach is asserted. Existing book count is a catalog count, not proof of readers.

## Official criteria, conditions and credibility
BlueDot offers **$20,000–$200,000 for 6–12 months**, about **four weeks** decision time, approximately **30-minute** application, no fixed deadline stated. Supports books, writing, podcasts and videos, including a pivot by an established creator. Explicit tests: craft, accuracy, **existing self-built following**, and credible distribution growth. Applicant supplies work/channel links; review and possible call precede a grant agreement. Monthly short updates, final reach report, return of unspent funds if stopping early. Editorial control retained; no mandatory BlueDot mention; political lobbying/campaigning excluded. Part-time permitted. Company payee possible only after discussion. Geography/sanctions exclusions apply. Tax may be due. Grant is restricted media support, **not salary or unrestricted contract revenue**. Source https://bluedot.org/grants/media. Payer credibility: established BlueDot grantmaker with published Rapid Grant awards, https://blog.bluedot.org/p/weve-given-out-50000-in-rapid-grants ; this is not proof of Media Grant awards or acceptance odds.

## Conditional, non-submission project concept
A 6-month, original **AI systems and human control** reporting/explainer series derived from source-tracked Desk research, not recycled book chapters: four rigorously sourced long-form essays, four short accessible explainers and a publicly auditable corrections/source register. Editorial line: distinguish measured agent behavior from speculation, disclose limitations and funder, avoid unsupported catastrophe claims. Candidate outlets: Shelf reader and existing video channels **only if** their audience fit and rights are verified. Deliverables are proposed, not already produced.

Illustrative restricted budget **$40,000**: 300 founder research/writing hours × $60 = $18,000; 100 editorial/fact-check hours × $70 = $7,000 (no editor engaged or quote obtained); production/visuals $5,000; distribution/accessibility $4,000; primary-source research/travel $3,000; contingency $3,000. All line items hypothetical; do not represent as market quotes or funding needs already incurred. Verify founder availability, editor, rights, taxes and project necessity before any application.

## Economics and release gate
Economic benefit model, not grant face value: 300 funded founder hours × ($60 funded rate − $35 displaced-work value) = **$7,500** conditional pretax surplus, assuming funds legally support those hours; exclude other restricted expenses from founder benefit. Future eligibility/analytics/application verification 2h × $35 = **$70**. Subjective central fit 35% × award conditional 3% = **1.05%** joint; prospective EV $7,500 × .0105 − $70 = **+$8.75**. Conservative fit 20% × award 1% => **−$55**. Favorable fit 50% × award 5% => **+$117.50**. These are invented planning scenarios, **not measured selection probabilities**. Tax and delivery overrun unpriced; if $60/h is not permitted or personally available, EV must be recomputed. Incremental uplift from this preflight is **not measured**.

**Hard gate:** obtain first-party dated 90-day reach/subscriber/readership analytics for an actually controlled publication/channel, three best original clips with publication dates, rights and payee jurisdiction, proof of founder time and editorial capacity, nonoverlap with EA/other grants, and an explicit approval before applying. If no self-built following exists, remain WATCH and do not generate more application prose. Do not pay, contact, submit or accept terms without approval.
