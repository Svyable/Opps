## Risk-adjusted economics

**Award ceiling is not income or guaranteed profit.** $4,500 would be restricted research support; 60 hours of labor are an actual opportunity cost. The previously used **$1,800 conditional economic benefit** is an internal assumption, not grant face value. At $35/hour, a future 0.5h applicant review costs $17.50. Illustrative *not empirically estimated* joint probabilities: 50% eligible/fit × 60% deliverability × 8% selection = 2.4%. On conditional benefit $1,800, prospective EV = $1,800×0.024 − $17.50 = **+$25.70** *before* tax, funding overlap, IP/rights and the cost of future technical work not already reflected in the $1,800 benefit. Under a 20% fit stress case, EV = $1,800×0.20×0.60×0.08−$17.50 = **−$0.22**. These are sensitivity examples, not measured award probabilities or evidence of positive true EV. Break-even fit probability under those assumptions = $17.50/($1,800×0.60×0.08) ≈ **20.3%**. Actual expected cash value remains unknown.

**Decision:** restore conditional PURSUE for a **bounded applicant-controlled 30-minute eligibility/terms/nonoverlap review** because the current official page explicitly supports time/living costs and $4,500 is within the grant cap. No further speculative engineering or drafting until one release gate is evidenced. If the official page changes again, re-check it before application. No submission, spending, registration, contact or IP commitment.
