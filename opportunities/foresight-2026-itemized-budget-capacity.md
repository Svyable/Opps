## The decisive funding issue
The proposed $35,000 grant must buy **an empirical comparison of meaningful human control against oversight theatre**, not just an attractive open-source protocol. The sponsor requires open-sourcing funded code/data/outputs, prioritizes in-person contributions to its San Francisco or Berlin Nodes, accepts individuals and for-profit organizations (with a funding-need explanation), and limits overhead to **10% of direct research costs**. Successful applicants must pass due diligence, provide an itemized budget and project plan, accept public naming unless they arrange otherwise, and submit progress updates. See official FAQ, not third-party summaries.

**We have not verified** founder weekly capacity, payee/legal status, grant-eligible rights, contractors, reviewers, reference consent or Node participation. All hourly rates and budgets below are *estimates*, not offers or payroll commitments.

### What the funder would receive
- **Weeks 1–2:** frozen policies, sandbox threat model, safety and data handling plan, holdout and baseline preregistration.
- **Weeks 3–5:** reproducible three-arm harness (no gate; nominal approval; enforced authorization boundary) and synthetic task set, no real secrets or external effects.
- **Weeks 6–8:** randomized trials, attack cases, raw per-arm denominators and failure trace receipts. Report task failures rather than dropping them.
- **Weeks 9–10:** blinded review of held-out cases, disagreement resolution, confidence intervals, false blocks and throughput costs.
- **Weeks 11–12:** open-source release, exact reproduction instructions, a decision rubric and limitations, and final sponsor report.

**No completed experiments or third-party validation are asserted.** At least 60 sandbox task specifications are a proposed minimum, not a power calculation. If sample sizes cannot support a stable inference, report exploratory estimates and uncertainty instead of significance.

Only use if the applicant is in fact applying as a for-profit and can truthfully support the statements.

## Applicant-controlled stop/go checklist
1. **Payee and tax status:** legal recipient, jurisdiction and any organizational documents; no invented entity.
2. **Capacity:** select *one* budget based on demonstrated 20h/week or 12h/week availability. If neither is true, do not promise either.
3. **Engineering/reviewer procurement:** actual willing personnel, availability, scope, rates and any conflicts; otherwise label estimates and adjust delivery scope.
4. **Open-source rights:** licenses, employee/contractor assignment, third-party data permissions and privacy handling. Funded code, data and outputs must be releasable.
5. **Node fit:** state *actual* ability to work from San Francisco/Berlin Nodes or attend dedicated sprints; do not assert location or participation from a guessed residence.
6. **Professional reference:** named consenting reference; not fabricated.
7. **Separate from EA Funds TAIF:** Foresight pays prospective intervention-value experiments; TAIF would pay retrospective claim-to-trace adjudication. Separate staff hours, receipts, costs, outputs and reporting; no double funding.
8. **Application terms:** review live Airtable fields, sharing/consent choices, due diligence and privacy; applicant revises narrative personally and explicitly approves submission.
