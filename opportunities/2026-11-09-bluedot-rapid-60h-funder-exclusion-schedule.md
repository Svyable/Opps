No contract/acceptance terms have been reviewed.

## Economic decision
$4,500 is restricted proposed research support, not founder profit. If all 60 hours are genuinely incremental, illustrative opportunity cost at $35/h is **$2,100**, leaving **$2,400 pretax before fees, taxes, overhead and restrictions** if fully funded and paid; this is *not* an established surplus because $35/h is only a screening assumption. At 30% joint qualification/feasibility and 8% conditional award probability, expected conditional $2,400 surplus is **$57.60** before applicant work. At $17.50 review cost, hypothetical net **$40.10**; at 10% fit, net **$1.70**. These are sensitivity calculations, **not measured selection odds**, and can turn negative with taxes or higher opportunity cost. Actual EV remains unknown. A zero-spend stop is appropriate until first-party gates clear.
