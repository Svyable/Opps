# City of San José PUR-RFP2026.09.10071 — AI Reporting Services

**Status:** REJECT  
**Deadline:** October 22, 2026  
**Value:** $50k–$300k third-party estimate; not a payer-stated ceiling.

Reported requirements involve secure police reporting workflows, body-worn-camera data, RMS/CAD integration, and future Axon integrations.

## Fit

Existing Svyable agent/evaluation work is not enough to offset the need for a mature public-safety enterprise platform and integrations.

## Decision

REJECT as a direct bid. New-work cost and delivery risk are too high relative to reuse and realistic win probability.
