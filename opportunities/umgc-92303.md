## Commercial decision

**Do not invest in a full prime bid.** The conservative hypothetical prime EV is **−$1,800**: $300k potential task orders × 35% contribution margin × 50% eligibility × 8% selection − $6k bid cost. All inputs except the $1m ceiling and ~$100k typical order are assumptions. It does not account for the zero-minimum order risk, so it may be optimistic.

A **narrow specialist subcontract** in evidence-backed AI content QA could be commercially plausible under a qualified prime, but only if the buyer permits the arrangement, an actual prime exists, and delivery economics and IP rights are agreed. A cautious illustrative marginal EV is **−$42**; favorable conditional scenario **+$284.38**, both before any further teaming expense.
