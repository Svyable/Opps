## Positioning

Svyable should pitch **evidence-backed AI content operations**, not generic AI writing.

Each learning object should carry:
1. explicit skill target;
2. source packet and provenance;
3. lesson/example/simulation/assessment;
4. human QA state;
5. measurable learner task;
6. update trigger;
7. source → claim → objective → assessment traceability;
8. version history.

## Proposed pilot

A 4–6 week stackable module: **Applied AI Evaluation and Agent Reliability**.

Possible sequence:
- what AI systems can and cannot prove;
- prompt/tool-use failure modes;
- reproducible evaluation sets;
- bounded agent permissions and human authorization;
- provenance/evidence packaging;
- regression testing after model/prompt changes;
- capstone audit of an AI workflow.
