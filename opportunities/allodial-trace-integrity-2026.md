## Published opportunity

The Allodial Foundation's Fiduciary Overlay Research Program offers **up to three** fixed-price, **12-week** research engagements, **up to $50,000 each**, on a rolling basis. The call may close when the award pool is committed. Individuals and organizations can apply. Track 03 asks for methods that compare assistants' completed-action claims against tool/runtime traces.

The sponsor requests a reference-free runtime method, working Python implementation, corpus-level distributions with uncertainty, stable I/O schemas by week four, validation and engineering handoff. Any learned runtime component must use an openly available self-hostable model. The sponsor describes a permissive open-source requirement and researchers retaining authorship/publication rights.

**Published milestone schedule:** 20% kickoff; 30% week four; 30% week ten; final 20% upon holdout acceptance. The holdout acceptance rubric and number of remaining awards are not published.
