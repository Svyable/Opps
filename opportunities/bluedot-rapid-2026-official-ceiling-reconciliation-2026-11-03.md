# BlueDot Rapid — official ceiling and budget reconciliation (2026-11-03)

**Internal decision artifact; not an application or approval to spend.**

## Verified official source discrepancy

- [BlueDot Rapid program-specific page](https://bluedot.org/grants/rapid) currently states **$50–$10,000** grants, usually upfront, sometimes reimbursed; specific project costs preferred; no fixed application deadline shown. It says stipends/living expenses are *not usual scope* and require a stronger case.
- [BlueDot grants overview](https://bluedot.org/grants) currently advertises Rapid **up to $20,000**. These official pages disagree; for a Rapid proposal, use the more specific **$10,000** page as the conservative ceiling until funder clarification. Do not present $20,000 as an unqualified Rapid maximum.
- [BlueDot April 14, 2026 grantmaking report](https://blog.bluedot.org/p/weve-given-out-50000-in-rapid-grants) reports 77 awards totaling over $50,000; useful payer-history evidence, not approval of this proposal.
- [BlueDot technical sprint reimbursement page](https://blog.bluedot.org/p/rapid-small-grants-for-the-bluedot) is a **different restricted sprint-participant scheme**: $50–$500 compute-type reimbursements, no time compensation. Do not conflate it with the general Rapid program.

## Impact on Svyable's existing $4,500 alternative

The prior self-contained proposal requests **60 founder hours × $75/h = $4,500**. The arithmetic is valid and amount is below both stated ceilings, but **the budget is almost entirely a stipend**, a category the current program-specific page says is *not usual scope*. This is a substantive funding-fit weakness, not a minor drafting issue. Do not claim the $4,500 is ordinary approved tooling expenditure, or convert it to fictional cloud/API invoices.

**Stop gate:** applicant must establish whether the 60-hour time-support case is exceptional and eligible, or identify actual documented project costs. A no-stipend alternative cannot be invented without genuine invoices/quotes and a real technical need. No contact with BlueDot was made.

## Conservative economics and choice

Conditional founder economic surplus from a restricted grant is not the grant face value. The previous marginal central scenario ($1,800 conditional benefit × 60% feasible × 8% selected − $35 applicant labor = **+$51.40**) becomes **−$35** if the time-support request is ineligible and no other actual eligible costs exist (qualification probability effectively zero). A purely illustrative 10% probability that an exception is accepted, holding other assumptions constant, gives **−$26.36**: $1,800 × 10% × 60% × 8% − $35. These are scenario assumptions, not empirical odds. This memo adds estimated 0.5h × $35 = $17.50 in internal labor, not counted in the future-only figures. There is no measured uplift.

**Decision:** retain PURSUE provisionally because the funder explicitly invites stronger stipend cases, but do not submit or undertake more funded work until the applicant verifies lawful receipt, contributor rights, no overlapping funder costs, genuine 60-hour availability, and the rationale why a stipend is indispensable. If those gates fail, downgrade to WATCH or REJECT. The $6,000 alternative remains separate and unapproved. Reuse the existing Control Evidence prototype and historical tests; no new code was necessary to identify this blocker.
