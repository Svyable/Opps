## Official rule, verified today
[BlueDot Media Grants](https://bluedot.org/grants/media): $20,000–$200,000 for 6–12 months; approximately four-week decision; application about 30 minutes. Review explicitly considers **craft, accuracy, audience and distribution**. The FAQ says an applicant **needs a track record of good work and a following they built themselves**, though the following need not be large or focused on AI. For creators just starting out, BlueDot directs them to Rapid Grants. BlueDot offers editorial independence, permits part-time proposals, requires monthly and final reporting, and expects unspent funds returned if the project stops. Grant can be taxable and is not a services contract. Country/sanctions restrictions and final grant terms require checking.
