## Sponsor rule that changes the application decision

BlueDot's [canonical Seed page](https://bluedot.org/grants/seed) says the award funds the **first 6–12 months of a new project OR organization**, from **$20,000–$200,000**, and explicitly accepts **solo founders**. It instructs applicants to choose **“Starting an organization”** in the form. This **form routing label is not evidence that an applicant must already have incorporated a new entity**. Existing Svyable infrastructure can be background work for a genuinely distinct *new* SDK/adoption project; it must not be represented as a newly formed organization, newly developed code, new users, or already funded research. Whether this particular proposal qualifies remains BlueDot's decision.

BlueDot's [grant overview](https://bluedot.org/grants) lists restricted recipient jurisdictions and sanctions; **no jurisdiction, nationality or tax status is inferred from the GitHub owner**. BlueDot generally pays the **individual founder**; payment to an organization requires discussion, and for-profit projects are considered case by case. This is a **grant, not a services contract, investment or salary**; a signed agreement precedes payment, monthly updates and a final report are required, and unspent funds are returned if the project stops. The site shows no fixed deadline; the application form's conditional fields have not been inspected. **Do not contact the funder or submit without authorization.**

## Applicant-only go/no-go checklist (do not publish personal answers)

1. **New project:** Can the applicant truthfully describe a *new SDK/integration and adoption effort* with deliverables not already delivered or funded? If not, **no-go**. Existing Svyable is not automatically a new organization.
2. **Time:** Is approximately **35 hours/week for six months** genuinely available, including any competing grant and paid work obligations? If not, rescope the $45k runway and entire budget before application.
3. **Payee:** Can the actual individual founder lawfully receive a grant in their jurisdiction, handle tax/reporting, and accept the agreement? If organization payment is required, terms are **unconfirmed**.
4. **Rights:** Confirm actual copyright/contributor ownership and notices for any copied files; exclude unlicensed `agents` material and ScrolIQ scientific dependencies from the initial SDK. Clear the chosen external framework's license and permission to integrate.
5. **Evidence:** No external user, independent reviewer, security certification, real-agent efficacy or consent is verified. Make no such claim.
6. **Funding overlap:** Disclose relevant applications/awards accurately, never charge past work or the same hours twice.
7. **Authority:** Applicant reviews exact form, supplies their own identity, signs off on proposed statements and explicitly authorizes submission. This audit does not authorize outreach, expenses or agreement acceptance.
