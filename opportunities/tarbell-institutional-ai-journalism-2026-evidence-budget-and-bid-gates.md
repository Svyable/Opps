Tarbell's institutional page requires the *specific wording and format* of funding disclosures to be approved before publication. This is an approval obligation if funded, not permission to submit now.
- [October 5 launch announcement](https://www.tarbellcenter.org/blog/tarbell-launches-10-million-institutional-grants-program-to-expand-ai-journalism) names NPR and Machine Gods as recipients. Official case studies identify $250k and $480k grants respectively. This is concrete payer credibility, **not** a comparable selection rate for an unknown one-person outlet.
- Separate [individual reporting grants](https://www.tarbellcenter.org/grants) ($1k–$20k) **closed July 12, 2026**. Do not confuse that closed intake with this open institutional round.

## Exact release gates and stop rule
1. **Fundable outlet/payee:** Sven verifies a lawful recipient able to accept grant funding, taxation, banking, relevant publication ownership and editorial independence. Public GitHub ownership is not proof of legal payee.
2. **Audience evidence:** retrieve dated unique readers, referrals, downloads, subscribers, newsletter/open rates, actual reader feedback and any third-party syndication. If none, state zero verified audience rather than invent it; outline a credible distribution plan.
3. **Journalism evidence:** identify 2–3 real independent original reporting samples and firsthand source access, or explicitly disclose none. Books and source ledgers alone do not meet Tarbell's preference for impressive reporting.
4. **Capacity and contractors:** personally affirm ~500h over 12 months, editor/fact-checker availability, reporting expense realism and project budget.
5. **Rights/conflicts:** author approves reuse of specified research; identify third-party rights and conflicts involving Svyable's AI projects; reconcile overlap with BlueDot, EA Funds and other grants. No double-billing.
6. **Applicant authorization:** personally inspect the live Airtable form, grant terms, data sharing, AI disclosure and privacy requirements. No application, terms acceptance or outreach without explicit approval.
