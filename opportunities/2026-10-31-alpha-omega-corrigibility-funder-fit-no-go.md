## Secondary screen: Corrigibility Research Fund Round 2

**Official sources:** [Corrigibility Research Fund](https://corrigibilityresearch.org/), [manager's September 3 selection methodology](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/q2YL7qKigC9QEEdsX/how-i-m-evaluating-corrigibility-grant-applications), [round-one grantees](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/CKArJ4GAQGFjnhjx6/corrigibility-research-fund-grantees-round-1). Checked 2026-10-31.

- **Value and deadline:** $5,000–$35,000 typical grants, October 31, 2026 Round 2 cutoff (no exact hour confirmed). The manager reports eight first-round grants totaling $132,000 from more than 100 applications; this is **funder-reported historical grantmaking**, not Svyable eligibility. The program is operated by Lightcone Infrastructure, a 501(c)(3); grants are charitable, not business capital, and applications on Lightcone Commons should be treated as public.
- **Decisive research-fit weakness:** the manager explicitly says *externally forced obedience, shutdown, or tool control* is not the agent's **voluntary corrigible disposition**. Svyable's current Control Evidence receipt/authenticity mocks measure whether a separate mechanism accepts or rejects action claims; they do **not** measure whether an agent proactively reveals its own mistakes, seeks correction, or changes its goals. Renaming those mocks as corrigibility research would be misleading. The manager also flags generic AI-written, funder-shaped applications as a negative signal.
- **Reuse / work:** Human Kind's authority concepts and the existing Control Evidence harness could serve as a control baseline, but a genuinely new behavioral or conceptual study would require an independently justified research question, real agent experiments or a serious philosophical argument, and original researcher judgment. At least ~2 hours application work and much more research delivery; applicant capacity unverified.
- **Subjective EV:** $4,000 hypothetical surplus after grant obligations × 5–15% research-fit/capacity × 1–3% conditional selection − $70 (2h × $35) = **−$68 to −$52**. Prize nominations for completed 2026 corrigibility research are not an alternative: current synthetic tests are not shown to advance scientific understanding of corrigibility.
- **Decision:** **REJECT Round 2** for this asset set. Do not submit a superficial adaptation, disclose private source, or claim original behavioral results. Reconsider only after independent research with a real corrigibility-specific insight and a truthful applicant-written argument.
