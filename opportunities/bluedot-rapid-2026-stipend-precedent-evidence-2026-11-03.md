## Honest project-specific exception test

The existing $4,500 request is 60 founder hours at $75/h. A credible exception would require all of:
1. A verifiable current funding bottleneck: explain why those 60 **future** hours cannot otherwise be funded or delivered, and why existing volunteer/code work does not solve it.
2. Distinct prospective deliverables: run-bound recorder authentication/replay tests, adversarial negative controls, reproducible benchmarks and a short public report. Existing 82-test historical CI is prior work, not a funded deliverable.
3. A defensible $75/h rate grounded in actual applicant compensation/contracting evidence or reduced to an evidence-based amount; do not invent quotes or invoices.
4. An explicit AI-safety impact path, including realistic limitations: local synthetic receipt integrity is not independent agent attestation and does not by itself demonstrate catastrophic-risk reduction.
5. Applicant-controlled checks: legal payee/jurisdiction, contributor/license rights, 15h/week for four weeks, and no double-funding of the same hours by EA Funds, BlueDot Seed or another award.
6. An applicant-authored explanation of why **founder time**, rather than compute/tooling, is the marginal blocker. No invented expenses or fabricated external validation.
