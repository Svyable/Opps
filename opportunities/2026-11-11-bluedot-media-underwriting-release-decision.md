## Cash decision and sensitivity (illustrations, not forecasts)
At the **minimum** $20,000 request, proposed 260h × $35/h opportunity cost = $9,100, leaving $10,900 before production expenses, overhead and taxes. Eight hours to prepare a full application costs $280. Thus EV of that *full* pursuit would be `p × ($10,900 − production costs) − $280`. At zero other costs, break-even **p > 2.57%**; at $2,000 production costs, **p > 3.15%**. These are required *joint eligibility/selection/payment probabilities*, not estimates. The true probability, incremental uplift, and monetary EV are **unknown**; a grant is restricted support, not guaranteed profit. An initial two-hour/$70 owner-evidence check would need an approximately **0.64 percentage-point** increase in the probability of realizing the conditional $10,900 margin to pay for itself (assuming no other expenses); no such increase is established.
