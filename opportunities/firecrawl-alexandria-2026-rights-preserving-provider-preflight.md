# Firecrawl Alexandria — rights-preserving Sven Hardy Benson content-provider preflight

**Research: 2026-10-31 · Status: WATCH · Internal/nonbinding · No provider application, content transfer, registration, or license granted.**

## What is actually available

Firecrawl announced Alexandria on **September 22, 2026**. Its own [announcement](https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/introducing-alexandria-series-b) says it already pays selected providers under individual agreements (including Wikimedia Enterprise), and plans a self-service contribution system. The live [Alexandria provider page](https://www.firecrawl.dev/alexandria) says **join the provider waitlist**. It does **not** publish a standard contributor royalty, minimum guarantee, conversion rate, acceptance deadline, payment schedule, or a generally available self-service contract. Its announced $75m financing is company financing, **not** a licensing budget offered to Svyable. Its 21% answer-quality figure is Firecrawl's own internal benchmark, not an evaluation of Desk.

**Why this is a plausible existing-asset match:** Firecrawl wants original first-party knowledge and publisher content; [Sven Hardy Benson's Desk](https://github.com/Svyable/desk) holds original nonfiction and associated research ledgers. Desk reports **137 projects, 65 complete drafts and 57 Shelf releases** in its README. Those counts are repository-reported, not an independently audited rights-cleared corpus. The only works inspected individually for this packet are below.

## Nonbinding potential commercial package (not an offer)

**Potential product**: a queryable, attributed *Sven Hardy Benson original analysis* collection on AI delegation, machine-mediated markets, and education. Its potential distinction is original authored synthesis with versioned sources and explicit caveats, not exclusive ownership of underlying public-domain laws or third-party research.

**First negotiating position, only if the author approves a discussion:** limited retrieval rights for a named subset of works and defined outputs; per-query revenue share or minimum guarantee; transparent usage reports and audit; copyright/author attribution; no training/fine-tuning, model-weight incorporation, bulk resale, unlimited sublicensing, or derivative publication; enforceable deletion/revocation and access controls; term/territory/caps; third-party source carve-outs; data-security and tax/payment review. No clause is accepted, and these conditions are not known to be offered by Firecrawl. A buyer might reject them.

**Do not** upload manuscript files, publish a public embedding index, expose full-text search, register a provider, send a waitlist form, or negotiate on behalf of the author without explicit approval. An unlicensed free public feed could cannibalize future book/audio/translation rights.

## Decision economics (all screening assumptions, no published payout rate)

Revenue is **unknown** until provider pricing and demand are disclosed. Treat $0 as the verified currently contracted revenue. For a *one-hour/$35* internal review of this opportunity, an illustrative one-year economic contribution after delivery and fees (not gross Firecrawl credit spend) is:

| Scenario | P(rights/fit) | P(agreement given fit) | P(paid demand given agreement) | Conditional annual contribution | Marginal EV |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cautious | 40% | 5% | 20% | $1,000 | **−$31** |
| Central | 50% | 10% | 20% | $2,000 | **−$15** |
| Favorable | 70% | 20% | 30% | $4,000 | **+$133** |

Formula: `EV = P(fit) × P(deal|fit) × P(usage|deal) × contribution − $35`. These probabilities, contribution figures and labor rate are **hypothetical**, not observed. Future outreach, legal review, integration and rights opportunity costs are **excluded**, so even the favorable figure is not a complete deal EV. No improvement in agreement probability is claimed from this packet. A 1-hour preflight is defensible as reusable IP diligence; **do not spend additional labor on integration before an actual commercial term sheet**.

## Decision gates

1. **Author:** affirm ownership, coauthor/third-party permissions and whether any of these specific works may be licensed for AI retrieval. No assumption that public content is freely reusable.
2. **Payer:** obtain real provider royalty/usage terms, reporting, indexing/training rights, minimums, termination and data deletion. Current official page offers only a waitlist.
3. **Economics:** compare expected net contribution against sales/translation/audio/exclusive-rights opportunity cost, support burden, legal fees and tax.
4. **Approval:** only after explicit approval may an inquiry/waitlist, agreement negotiation, data delivery or license grant occur.

**Decision: WATCH / commercial option, not yet a paying opportunity.** The concrete progress is a rights-audited, non-disclosive candidate catalog that avoids the largest preventable mistake—treating All Rights Reserved manuscripts as free AI data. Recheck when provider terms become public or the author authorizes a licensing discussion.
