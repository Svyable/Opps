## Copy-ready application — four platform sections
### What are you working on?
Svyable develops evidence-to-decision tools that distinguish a plausible claim from evidence that independently supports it. In our existing Control Evidence prototype, identical file contents can be produced by an authorized action or an adversarial injection. We therefore label the observed artifact `OBSERVED_ONLY` instead of asserting that an authorized agent acted. Our scorer and adversarial tests make this distinction testable. We propose applying the same discipline to claims, sources, and decisions: an inspectable provenance trail and comparative evaluation of when reviewers mistakenly accept unsupported claims. We are not proposing a centralized arbiter of truth.

### What would you do with funding?
A 12-week, $50,000 **provisional** pilot: (1) define and document a claim–source–evidence schema; (2) construct a rights-cleared set of supported, unsupported and adversarial examples; (3) adapt the existing scorer into a reproducible baseline-versus-evidence-assisted evaluation; (4) pre-register paired comparisons, minimum strata and uncertainty intervals; (5) publish a small demonstrator, results, limitations and reusable evaluation code. Claims of authenticated execution will be excluded unless independent recorder identity and key custody are demonstrated. Funding would support engineering, independent evaluation, data rights review, participant testing and infrastructure; budget figures remain estimates pending quotes.
