# BlueDot Rapid — eligibility evidence and go/no-go decision (2026-11-09)

**Opportunity:** https://bluedot.org/grants/rapid — conditional PURSUE; proposed $4,500 for 60 *future* Control Evidence feasibility hours. Internal evidence gate, **not an application**.

## New first-party eligibility check
The official page (read 2026-11-09) states: (1) awards up to $20,000; (2) research time and living costs are allowable; (3) it funds promising next steps in AI safety/biosecurity; (4) BlueDot course participants, alumni, facilitators and active members of the AI safety/biosecurity community are **more likely** to receive a grant, but membership is not mandatory; (5) restricted payout jurisdictions and sanctions apply; (6) the grant is paid through a claims portal after checks, and completion reporting is required within 60 days of finishing. No fixed deadline or published award odds/decision SLA. These conditions matter more than further synthetic-code polish.

## Explicit economic stop/go

Payer credibility: BlueDot publishes a grant program, requirements and a claims process; this is **not** an award promise. Gross request $4,500; net conditional founder benefit unknown. Illustrative application-only screening (not empirical): conditional economic surplus $1,800 × P(eligible and feasible) 0.30 × P(award | eligible and feasible) 0.08 − 0.5h × $35 = **+$25.70**. At P(eligible and feasible)=0.10, EV **−$3.10**. If founder surplus is zero, EV is negative regardless of award odds. The assumptions are unverified; no reliable positive cash EV can be claimed.

**Decision:** preserve conditional PURSUE but stop further proposal/code expansion. Next high-value action is an applicant-controlled, dated eligibility attestation and a nonoverlap funding election, then a short submission review *only with explicit approval*. The proposed 60h are not already delivered or eligible for retroactive reimbursement. No spend, contact, application, or rights transfer.
