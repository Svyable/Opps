# BlueDot Seed Grants — founder eligibility and commercial fit reconciliation (2026-11-10 UTC)

**Decision: WATCH, conditional promotion only after first-party founder/capacity and public-benefit evidence.** This is a pre-submission eligibility assessment, not an application, approval or grant agreement.

## Direct official evidence (retrieved 2026-11-10 UTC)
- [Seed Grants official terms](https://bluedot.org/grants/seed): $20,000–$200,000 for 6–12 months; approximately four-week decision, no fixed submission cutoff stated; ~45-minute application, selecting **“Starting an organization”**; follow-up call and grant agreement before payment.
- **Material clarification:** solo founders explicitly welcome; BlueDot course participation not required; **for-profit companies may qualify in some cases where work clearly serves the public good**, with longer approvals. **Default Seed payment is to individual founders**, not automatically the company; organization payee must be negotiated. It is a grant, not a salary, investment, or services contract; may be taxable. No equity or IP license is specified on the public page; full agreement is not available and must be reviewed before acceptance.
- Selection evidence: problem selection, well-reasoned hypothesis, demonstrated prior work, concrete initial milestones. Project must be intended to grow beyond one person. Monthly updates, final report and return of unspent funds on early cessation. Restricted payment jurisdictions and sanctions apply.
- [BlueDot general grants](https://bluedot.org/grants) confirms Seed as distinct from Rapid; [BlueDot funded examples](https://bluedot.org/grants/seed) include Recurrent Research, Golemet and others. These establish named examples, not selection odds.

## Svyable-specific qualification gates
Existing Control Evidence receipt verification, funding-overlap checks, agent-evaluation research and related code provide reusable early technical work. **Not established:** whether this is genuinely a *new* organization/project rather than a relabeled existing product; whether it will grow beyond founder; founder legal jurisdiction, 6–12 month committed availability, public-benefit theory of change tied to catastrophic advanced-AI risk, actual independent-recorder results, authorized rights to all reused IP, credible external user validation, or a conflict-free allocation against the proposed $4,500 BlueDot Rapid and $25,000 EA Funds budgets. No claim of full-time availability is made. The official page says living costs may enable full-time work, but does not expressly impose a universal 35h/week threshold.

## Commercial economics — scenario, not a probability forecast
At a *hypothetical* $25,000 award, 6 months × 20h/week × 26 weeks at $35/hour = $18,200 opportunity cost; conditional economic margin = $6,800 before taxes, overhead and additional costs. At a $70 application-review cost, **EV = $6,800 × p(joint eligibility, award and payment) − $70**. Break-even p ≈ **1.03%**. No defensible estimate of p exists. At 35h/week the same $25k award would not cover hypothetical $31,850 labor cost; therefore a larger award or lower scope would be necessary. $20k–$200k is a published grant range, not an entitlement or contract price. A future budget must be based on actual deliverables, not an invented rate.

## One concrete advancement / release checklist
This memo removes the **incorrect inference that Seed requires a cofounder, nonprofit incorporation, BlueDot alumni status, or a published 35h/week minimum**; it also flags a previously underemphasized **individual-payee / taxable-grant / public-good-for-profit** approval gate. To promote WATCH→PURSUE, require (1) first-party founder and payment-jurisdiction attestation; (2) a genuinely new 6–12-month project and evidence it can grow beyond founder; (3) non-overlapping deliverables and budget; (4) independent technical evidence and at least one real intended user or evaluator; (5) draft budget whose expected payout exceeds delivery costs; (6) agreement/IP/tax review. No application, contact, spending, or acceptance authorized.

**Stop rule:** do not spend additional autonomous proposal/engineering hours until at least one first-party gate is resolved. Reassess if official rules or validated project evidence changes.
