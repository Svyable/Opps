# TAIF applicant eligibility: official-rule reconciliation and submission decision (2026-11-21)

**Opportunity:** Existing Transformative AI Fund / Control Evidence $25,000 proposal. **Decision:** PURSUE / HOLD. This is a pre-submission verification artifact, not an application or evidence of award.

## Material correction to the existing blocker

The [official Transformative AI Fund page](https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/funds/transformative-ai) explicitly accepts **individuals, new organizations and existing organizations launching new projects**. The [official funding application page](https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/apply-for-funding) says applications are always open and covers people, projects, nonprofits and institutions. Consequently, **formation of a new legal entity is NOT a prerequisite established by these published eligibility rules**. Earlier packets' phrase “legal applicant/payee” should be understood as an identity, jurisdiction, tax and payment-diligence verification requirement, not an assertion that Svyable must incorporate before applying.

This correction removes a *false mandatory incorporation dependency* from the decision tree. It does **not** prove Sven's personal eligibility, country eligibility, identity verification, tax treatment or grant-contract acceptability. The sponsor's complete application, due-diligence and grant terms have not been accepted or verified here.

## Economics and alternatives

TAIF official typical grant range **$10,000–$150,000**, rarely above $300,000; applications **rolling**, no fixed cutoff stated. [EA Funds](https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/) reports over $100m of historical grants. The $25k requested amount is *restricted project funding*, not profit. Existing Svyable code/research reuse is high. Incremental research and write-up estimate 0.5–1.0h at an **illustrative** $35/h = $17.50–$35, no external spend. If (purely hypothetically) $5,000 of legitimate retained benefit exists, the necessary *incremental* joint success probability increase is **0.35–0.70 percentage points**. Neither that benefit nor an uplift is measured: **monetary EV remains unknown**, not demonstrably positive.

[BlueDot Seed](https://bluedot.org/grants/seed) is a distinct WATCH route ($20k–$200k, 6–12 months); its published rules allow solo founders but specify individual founder payment, monthly updates, return of unspent funds and slower review of for-profit projects. It is not a drop-in replacement for the smaller TAIF pilot. The five frontier benchmark lanes remain tracked in [campaign](2026-11-07-frontier-five-benchmark-campaign.md), with cash prizes unverified. Do not confuse scores with income.
