## Primary-source crosswalk (facts verified; not an article)

1. [CFPB Circular 2022-03](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/circulars/circular-2022-03-adverse-action-notification-requirements-in-connection-with-credit-decisions-based-on-complex-algorithms/): ECOA/Regulation B requires **specific, accurate principal reasons** for adverse credit decisions even with opaque algorithmic models; “the model is too complicated” is not an exemption. Do not assert that this requires disclosure of model weights or source code.
2. [FTC landlord guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/using-consumer-reports-what-landlords-need-know): if a tenant-screening consumer report influenced a negative rental decision, the landlord must provide an adverse-action notice identifying the reporting agency and dispute/free-report rights. The reporting agency is **not necessarily the landlord's decision maker**. This notice is **not the same legal duty** as the ECOA-specific credit explanation.
3. [CFPB tenant-screening error guide](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/tenant-background-checks/review-your-rental-background-check/): examples of inaccurate, duplicate or incomplete eviction information and how applicants may dispute it. Do not infer prevalence from the examples.
4. [CMS-0057-F official fact sheet](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/cms-interoperability-prior-authorization-final-rule-cms-0057-f): beginning in 2026 **impacted payers** must give a **specific denial reason** for prior authorizations; expedited 72-hour and standard seven-day timelines apply to specified payers. The rule **does not apply to drug prior authorizations**. Verify implementation and plan-specific scope before any real case.
5. [Asterisk pitch guide](https://asteriskmag.com/pitch-guide): explain method, target length, credentials and sources in a few **human-authored** paragraphs. A reported prior pay rate does not override current negotiated terms.
