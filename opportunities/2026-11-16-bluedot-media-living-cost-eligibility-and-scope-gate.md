**Decision: WATCH; materially improved budget feasibility, NOT award eligibility or application readiness.** Official rules: https://bluedot.org/grants/media (checked 2026-11-16).

## Material correction to earlier funding analysis
The official Media Grants page explicitly permits **living costs** to allow more time on media work, alongside production costs, and explicitly allows **part-time** creators. A company may be considered as payee case-by-case. Therefore the earlier ledger's "$0 independently verified media expense" is a statement about *missing evidence*, **not** a rule that founder time is ineligible. This opens a potentially direct paid-author route without claiming that an unverified cost has been approved.

Official funding: **$20,000–$200,000 for 6–12 months**, decisions aimed within **about four weeks**, applications accepted on a rolling basis according to BlueDot's grant index. Official screening emphasizes **craft, accuracy, audience and distribution**. Existing following need not be large, but the applicant must show a track record and a following they built. No BlueDot course prerequisite. No lobbying/political campaigning. Monthly short progress updates and final reach report; return unspent funds if stopping early. Editorial independence stated; actual grant agreement, copyright, tax, jurisdiction and sanctions still require review. BlueDot reports past awards on its grants index (https://bluedot.org/grants), but selection odds are not published.

## Conditional budget model (illustration, not an expense claim)
- Proposed request: **$20,000**, BlueDot's published minimum for Media Grants.
- Example allocation: $15,000 founder living-cost support for 300 hours of dedicated editorial/reporting/production time across six months (illustrative $50/h), $3,000 independently quoted fact-checking/editorial services, $2,000 captioning/design/distribution. **All amounts are unverified planning assumptions, not vendor quotes, incurred expenses, pay commitments or allowable-cost determinations.**
- Actual eligible request must be reduced/reworked if founder availability, outside income, editorial scope, payee, grant agreement or nonoverlap cannot be verified. Do not spend against anticipated reimbursement.
- Labor sensitivity: 2h additional preflight at $35/h = $70. A hypothetical **$5,000 incremental net personal benefit** after displaced work/delivery costs would require >1.4% joint eligibility×award×payment probability just to cover preflight. This is **not** a probability estimate; actual monetary EV remains unknown.

## Applicant-controlled hard gates before application
1. Dated proof of self-built audience: analytics/export for Shelf, channels or publication (reach, engaged viewers/readers, publication dates). A catalog of books is **not** audience evidence.
2. Two original, publicly viewable reporting/writing samples with byline/rights, sourcing and corrections practices. Book concepts or an unpublished pilot do not suffice.
3. Confirm applicant's lawful residence/payee and BlueDot sanctions restrictions; 300 hours of genuine six-month media capacity, separately tracked from TAIF technical work.
4. Document a conflict-free rights and cost ledger for founder time and outside services; obtain quotes only with authorization and without commitment.
5. Review actual application, disbursement, IP, termination, reporting and tax terms before approval; do not apply or contact BlueDot autonomously.

**Decision rule:** If no credible audience or two original samples can be verified in a 2-hour zero-spend preflight, retain WATCH and stop further grant writing. If verified, evaluate an applicant-approved application using actual incremental benefit, not the $20k headline. No claim of acceptance or qualification.
