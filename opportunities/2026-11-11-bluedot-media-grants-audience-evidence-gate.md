## Economics and decision
**Illustrative request, not approved:** $20,000 (the published minimum) for six months. At a hypothetical 10 h/week × 26 weeks × $35/h, opportunity cost is **$9,100**, leaving **$10,900** before production cash costs, tax and overhead. Add illustrative 8 h × $35 = **$280** application/evidence preparation. Conditional monetary EV = p × ($10,900 − verified production cash costs) − $280. At zero other costs break-even p > **2.57%**. This is a *threshold*, not an estimated selection probability; the applicant's eligibility and selection odds are unmeasured. Do not claim positive EV. A $20k award is not profit; funds are restricted to the agreed charitable media work.

**Decision:** This potentially exceeds the $4,500 Rapid proposal in absolute surplus and reuses more publishing IP, but is less immediately actionable until authentic audience evidence is verified. Time-box first-party evidence collection to **two hours ($70 hypothetical)**; if no qualifying track record can be documented, leave WATCH and prioritize a smaller Rapid pilot rather than writing a speculative $20k application.
