## Quantified budget stress test (illustrative, NOT a quote)

Holding 24 hours of experimental judgments and 6 hours reserve constant gives **50 hours before dispute resolution**, costing **$5,000** at the assumed $100/hour. The $4,000 line is short by **at least $1,000**, plus any dispute time. If the six-hour reserve is used for disputes, no general reserve remains. This cannot be cured by renaming R1/R2 as ground-truth judges: they are blinded experimental reviewers.

Primary endpoint: false-support rate/difference on independently checked non-support cases; report R3 uncertainty, indeterminates, discordance and sensitivity. The newer two-adjudicator operator study is an **unfunded expansion option**, not the promised ten-week deliverable. Any choice to fund the expanded design requires a revised quote, reconciled sample, budget, outcome and explicit applicant approval.
