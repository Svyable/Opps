## Official-source reconciliation

1. **General Rapid Grants**, https://bluedot.org/grants/rapid (checked 2026-11-06): up to **$20,000**, explicitly permits **time, living costs, research access**, projects and experiments. Eligibility: people taking promising steps in AI safety or biosecurity, subject to jurisdiction/sanctions restrictions. BlueDot community participation **improves odds but is not stated as mandatory**. Applications take about 15 minutes; no fixed closing date published. Payment is a single lump sum **after claim and checks**; use restricted to agreed purposes; report within 60 days after completion.
2. **Technical AI Safety Project Sprint small grants**, https://blog.bluedot.org/p/rapid-small-grants-for-the-bluedot (published 2026-03-17): requires current or past **Sprint participation**, explicitly **excludes compensation for time**, and ordinarily funds $50–$500 in compute/experiments. **Do not use this older, narrower program's exclusion to reject the general Rapid Grants $4,500 research-time proposal.** Likewise, do not misrepresent general eligibility as proof of award likelihood.
3. BlueDot's April 14, 2026 expansion announcement https://blog.bluedot.org/p/weve-given-out-50000-in-rapid-grants documents actual previous grants and broader scope; **the live general program page controls current ceiling and terms**.
