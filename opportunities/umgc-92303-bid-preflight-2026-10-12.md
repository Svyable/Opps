## Commercial correction

Third-party summaries report a **$1,000,000 IDIQ ceiling, no guaranteed minimum**, and approximately $100,000 typical task orders. The previous $18k EV estimate treated gross revenue as payoff and omitted qualification and delivery costs.

Base illustrative model: **$300k possible revenue × 35% contribution margin × 50% qualification × 8% conditional win − $6k bid cost = −$1,800**. Breakeven conditional win probability is 11.43% under these assumptions. The assumptions are uncalibrated. Limit the current qualification preflight to two hours / $70, not a full bid.
