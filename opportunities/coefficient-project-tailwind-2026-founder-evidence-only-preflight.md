## Why this option deserves a bounded check

[Project Tailwind](https://coefficientgiving.org/tailwind/) seeks founders of initiatives directly reducing **global catastrophic risks from transformative AI**, not general responsible-AI software, consulting revenue, or productivity products. Its [live FAQ](https://coefficientgiving.org/tailwind/faqs/) specifies **$200,000–$2 million pre-seed research/planning grants over 9–18 months**; seed funding is $2m–$20m and is not the relevant ask for this preflight. Competitive founder/team salaries may be included in budgets, but **the grant is restricted project capital, not a personal cash prize or guaranteed salary**. Pre-seed can go to individuals without a legal entity; international applicants can be considered, subject to legal/payment checks. For-profit support requires charitable purpose, and the funder says it does not generally expect to fund ordinary non-PBC for-profits. No fixed deadline is published; intake may close if demand is high. [Get involved](https://coefficientgiving.org/tailwind/get-involved/).

**Payer:** Coefficient Giving (formerly Open Philanthropy) has an established grantmaking record. Its [2026-09-09 report](https://coefficientgiving.org/research/were-urgently-scaling-our-work-on-ai-and-biosecurity/) says it *committed* $351m across technical AI safety, security and fieldbuilding in 2025 and reported a $160m Resolution grant. These are funder-reported commitments, not independently audited proof of a Tailwind award to Svyable.

## Falsifiable founder feasibility gates — stop before writing an EOI if any fail

1. **Mission:** Founder can articulate a specific catastrophic-AI-risk pathway and how authentic action evidence changes an actual safety decision. General accountability rhetoric is not enough.
2. **Founder capacity:** Founder is willing and able to build/lead an organization for 9–18 months (and potentially years), including recruitment and grant administration. No such commitment has been verified.
3. **Distinctness:** Clarify whether this should be a new nonprofit, a team within an existing institute, or an existing Svyable project. Tailwind explicitly encourages considering existing organizations. Do not form an entity without approval.
4. **External validity:** At least one genuinely independent domain expert or prospective institutional evaluator would need to validate that the trust-boundary problem matters at relevant scale. No interviews, users, signed letters or partners are evidenced.
5. **Rights:** Verify contributor permissions, third-party materials, AI-assisted code obligations and any employment restrictions. Apache-2.0 repository labeling alone is not a legal chain-of-title finding.
6. **Non-overlap:** BlueDot Rapid's proposed **60h / $4,500** four-week recorder-authenticity pilot and EA Funds' proposed **$25k / 10-week** Control Evidence pilot must not be double-billed or represented as awarded. Other Mozilla/Seed/Foresight work and private support require applicant attestation. Tailwind would need *new, non-overlapping* institution-building and externally validated evaluation milestones.
7. **Form/consent:** Applicant must independently write the EOI; no AI-generated text. Review legal entity, charitable-purpose, fiscal/tax and data-processing conditions; obtain explicit authorization before submission.

**Potential testable work, not a promise:** (a) run-bound evidence verifier with a frozen attacker model and exact forged/replay denominators; (b) independently repeated tests on a real authorized agent workflow; (c) documented evaluator decision impact, including negative findings and non-adoption. No third-party testing, external recruitment, or deployment is authorized by this dossier.
