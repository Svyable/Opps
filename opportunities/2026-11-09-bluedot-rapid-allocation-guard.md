# BlueDot Rapid — cross-funder allocation integrity gate
**2026-11-09, 00:00 America/Chicago. Internal pre-application control. Not submitted.**

## Why this matters
The existing [BlueDot $4,500/60h packet](2026-11-08-bluedot-rapid-nonoverlap-application-packet.md) and [EA $25,000 proposal](2026-11-08-ea-control-evidence-25000-reviewer-packet.md) both mention recorder engineering and reproducibility. They cannot claim or pay for the same labor or deliverables. A narrative nonoverlap pledge is not enough once there are real hours.

**Material advancement:** committed [funding_overlap.py](../artifacts/control-evidence-2026/funding_overlap.py) and [10 unittest cases](../artifacts/control-evidence-2026/test_funding_overlap.py). Local execution: `python -m unittest -v` in the source directory, **10/10 PASS**. The validator detects duplicate work identity across sponsors and within one sponsor, rejects missing identity, negative hours and NaN amounts, and totals allocations by sponsor. It is a **guardrail**, not proof that actual hours occurred, that work packages are semantically distinct, that invoices are authentic, or that either grantmaker has approved cost allocation. Two differently worded descriptions of the same work can evade this key-based check. Human audit and sponsor disclosures remain mandatory.

Usage: `python funding_overlap.py approved_allocations.json`; zero exit status means no detected collisions, not sponsor approval. Do **not** include hypothetical budgets as actual funded allocations; the test dataset is synthetic. Each actual funded record must have a stable package/date/contributor/deliverable/funder, positive hours, nonnegative dollar amount. Include only approved, funded, traceable costs. For current unfunded applications the actual allocations list should be empty.
