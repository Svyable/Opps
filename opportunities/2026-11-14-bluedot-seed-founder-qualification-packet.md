## Verified first-party rules (checked 2026-11-14)

Source: https://bluedot.org/grants/seed

- **$20,000–$200,000**, first **6–12 months**; ~45-minute application and ~4-week target decision; no fixed application deadline displayed.
- A **solo founder can apply**; prior BlueDot course participation is **not required**.
- **For-profit companies are possible in some cases**, if public-good work is clear, with longer approval.
- Seed grants are presently **paid to individual founders**; organization payment may be discussed but is not promised.
- Monthly short progress updates and final report; **unspent funds must be returned** if work stops.
- Signed grant agreement before payment; grant often taxable, and is **not salary, investment, or services contract**.
- Country/sanctions exclusions on official page; applicant jurisdiction and payee eligibility **not checked**.
- The fund assesses problem selection, hypothesis, execution evidence, and a concrete initial plan. Its page names funded organizations, supporting payer credibility but not any applicant's odds.

## Eligibility evidence request (internal only; no external contact)

1. Founder identity, jurisdiction, sanctions and ability to receive funds as an individual.
2. Verified capacity to devote a meaningful 6–12 months; founder's other obligations and employer IP restrictions.
3. Original ownership, contributor licenses, commercial/open-source rights, privacy/data permissions for existing artifacts.
4. Specific *new* public-good venture scope, intended users, adoption path, governance, and a genuinely nonoverlapping work allocation.
5. Itemized expenses with quotes/rates, payee, timing, tax treatment and cash-flow implications; **$0 verified allowable expenses at this audit**.
6. Confirm grant agreement, IP/license, termination, reporting, clawback and for-profit restrictions before any acceptance.

## Conservative economics and selection

Nominal gross grant range: **$20k–$200k**, restricted project support, **not founder profit**. Qualification probability and award probability **unknown**, not guessed. No closing date confirmed. Work to prepare a defensible application: at least a scoped budget, rights inventory, verified availability and independent technical evidence; hours unmeasured. Existing code reuse **high**, but legitimate new-project novelty **unverified**. Payer credible based on published program and named funded organizations. Terms carry reporting, return-of-unspent-funds, possible tax and individual-payee exposure.

Illustration only: at $35/h, a 2-hour eligibility check costs $70. If (and only if) the grant yields $5,000 in genuinely retainable incremental economic value, break-even *joint* eligibility/award/payment probability is 70/5000 = **1.4%**. Neither $5,000 nor that probability is established; actual cash EV is **unknown**, and could be negative after delivery cost and restrictions.

## Decision

**WATCH / no application** until founder commitment, exclusive project scope, actual eligible budget and IP rights are documented. Compared with the rolling EA Transformative AI Fund ($10k–$150k typical, https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/funds/transformative-ai), Seed offers higher nominal funding but materially higher commitment, novel-project and tax/contract exposure. Compared with BlueDot Rapid (up to $20k, https://bluedot.org/grants/rapid), Seed is not appropriate for a small overlapping pilot. Next decision: demonstrate an independently useful *non-TAIF* Seed venture and 6–12 month availability, or keep WATCH. No application, contact, spend, contract or rights transfer.
