2. **International measurement coordination.** On 13 February 2026, NIST reported consensus areas and open questions on automated AI evaluation from a ten-jurisdiction network. The proposed receipt would help compare evidence; it is **not** itself a compliance certificate. [EU AI Act Article 55](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-55).
4. **Netherlands policy relevance.** The Dutch government's 3 July 2026 international AI strategy prioritizes international cooperation, safety and European AI capacity. This is a potential policy audience, not evidence of procurement interest. [Government.nl](https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2026/07/03/international-strategy-for-safe-and-responsible-ai-transition).
5. **Cross-jurisdiction incident semantics.** OECD's 2025 common incident-reporting framework has 29 criteria and explicitly allows national adaptation. Its incident schema is adjacent to, but distinct from, model-evaluation evidence. [OECD paper](https://doi.org/10.1787/f326d4ac-en).

## Narrow policy-use case

Two independent evaluation teams report on the same model. A policy reviewer must decide whether the findings are comparable, materially incompatible, or too incomplete to transfer across jurisdictions. The proposed open **evidence receipt** captures model/version, evaluation objective, tested capability, dataset provenance, measurement method, scoring definition, uncertainty, evaluation date, disclosure restrictions, and reproducibility artifacts. It does **not** claim two legal systems have equivalent obligations.

## Testable study

Compare narrative-only summaries against structured receipts on matched cases. Primary metric: false-equivalence rate (incompatible results wrongly treated as comparable). Secondary metrics: appropriate abstention under missing data, unwarranted rejection of comparable evidence, review time, and reviewer confidence calibration. Pre-register case adjudication, blinded randomization, analysis and exclusion rules. Existing 20 synthetic cases are **pilot fixtures only**, not a held-out effectiveness trial; external reviewers and independent cases remain uncommitted.
