## Decision and commercial proposition

**Do not bid as prime.** Public Svyable work does not evidence two qualifying institutional client case studies, a current solution-specific VPAT 2.4+ ACR, Brightspace hosting/integration, learner outcomes, enterprise SLA, or an operational support desk. An eligible learning-delivery prime could potentially buy a narrow Svyable add-on: **AI Learning Content Evidence & Change-Control Layer**.

Proposed, not yet built for UMGC: (1) source-to-skill and assessment traceability; (2) versioned AI-assisted content change log; (3) human approval and rollback receipts; (4) reproducible QA and exception reports; (5) a privacy-preserving export. Existing [Desk](https://github.com/Svyable/desk), [Human Kind](https://github.com/Svyable/human-kind), and [ScrolIQ](https://github.com/Svyable/scrollq) supply research/code patterns, **not a production LMS service or verified university deployment**. Work would remain in the prime's controlled environment, with no independent hosting, student PII, accessibility attestation or service SLA promised by Svyable. Exact integration, access, security, ACR, ownership and support responsibilities must be contractually scoped after review.
