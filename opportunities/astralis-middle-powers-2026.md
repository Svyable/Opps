## Svyable-specific proposal

**Working title:** Reciprocal Evidence — Independent AI Evaluation Receipts for Middle Powers.

Develop a portable, open evidence format and reproducible test harness so Canadian and Dutch/EU evaluators can exchange safety-evaluation claims, model/version identifiers, provenance, limitations, and independent review decisions without depending on a frontier lab's unverified assertions. A narrowly scoped six-month demonstration would produce a Canada/Netherlands requirements crosswalk, schema, validation examples, two jurisdiction-specific policy briefs, and an independent technical review. Australia is an optional extension, not a promised partner.

- Desk's completed **The World Won't Choose Sides**: https://github.com/Svyable/desk/tree/main/books/the-world-wont-choose-sides

- Human Kind authority/evidence protocols: https://github.com/Svyable/human-kind
- ScrolIQ reproducible evaluation work: https://github.com/Svyable/scrollq

**Middle-power mechanism:** This is about cross-jurisdiction verification by third-party institutions in Canada and the Netherlands, not US domestic AI policy or generic AI safety tooling. The project would make evaluations produced by one jurisdiction inspectable and reusable in another, and report when that is not possible.
