## Commercial decision in one minute

- **Ask:** $35,000 for a 12-week, open-source prospective study of **no authorization gate vs cosmetic approval vs enforced tool-boundary authorization**. Foresight's published typical range is $30k–$100k; Human Empowerment grants may be smaller. The proposed request is **not** an award, customer payment, or unrestricted profit.
- **Reuse:** Existing [study manifest, deterministic mock boundary, paired-power calculator and synthetic pilot](../artifacts/foresight-control-2026/README.md), plus Human Kind authorization concepts and ScrolIQ evidence/provenance patterns. [Remote CI](https://github.com/Svyable/Opps/actions/runs/40635208184) reports **44 passing tests**. 36 of 60 synthetic cases map to 108 **mock** receipts. All 180 prospective AI-agent assignments remain unrun; no real human-approval or efficacy result exists.
- **Why this funder:** Foresight expressly seeks evidence, decision rules and mechanisms for distinguishing empowering human control from hollow oversight, with open-source results.
- **Modelled marginal EV (not a sponsor forecast):** Founder-led A: $12,000 conditional founder surplus × 45% delivery feasibility × 3.5% selection − $140 pursuit cost = **+$49**. At 25% feasibility, **−$35**. Delegated B: $7,200 × 65% × 3.5% − $140 = **+$23.80**; at 45% feasibility, **−$26.60**. Both selection and feasibility assumptions are subjective, and **neither route has verified staffing**. The economically rational next step is applicant fact verification, not another synthetic feature.
- **Decision:** **Conditional PURSUE until the deadline. STOP before submission if any mandatory attestation cannot be made truthfully.** If capacity/rights cannot be established, preserve the existing research and prioritize the rolling [EA Funds Transformative AI Fund](https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/funds/transformative-ai) draft instead. Do not misstate feasibility to beat a deadline.

## Claim and research-integrity release check

1. The existing mock gate, 108 deterministic receipts and 44 CI tests show **software behavior only**. No observed real-agent outcomes, authenticated human approvals, independent policy-label review or safety efficacy.
2. The 60 synthetic scenarios include only **20 policy-unauthorized matched cases per arm** for the primary contrast. [Power sensitivity](foresight-2026-paired-power-preflight.md) gives **17.36% illustrative power** under one assumed distribution, not a measured effect. Call the study **exploratory**, report uncertainty and counterexamples.
3. All 180 planned AI-agent assignments are unrun. The 24 unmapped fixture cases (72 planned assignments) are not silently represented by the 108 mock receipts.
4. The funder requires open-source funded work products, due diligence (including tax/organizational documentation and disclosures), regular progress reports, and normally public project/lead naming unless a privacy arrangement is made. The sponsor strongly prioritizes in-person Node contribution; remote-only is not a categorical exclusion.
5. A grant is restricted project support. Do not count the $35k face amount as profit or represent the 3.5% assumed selection probability as funder data.
