The founding-client offer

A decision brief that survives scrutiny.

From $750

One tightly framed question, researched and written for an actual decision. A typical commission is four to six substantial pages, with direct links to primary sources and a clear account of what the evidence does not establish.

  • The strongest evidence, with dates, provenance, and source links.
  • The competing case: what a skeptical, informed reader would object to.
  • Consequences, uncertainties, and the decisions worth separating.
  • One focused revision after the initial delivery.

Proposed turnaround: approximately five business days after agreed scope and materials, subject to availability. Final price, deadline, confidentiality requirements, and deliverables are confirmed in writing first. No payment is collected on this site.

Working terms

No mystery invoice. No generic report.

Start with the question and the decision it bears on. I will respond with a proposed scope and fee. If we agree, the deliverable is an original, source-linked memo with boundaries clearly stated. Sensitive or proprietary materials should not be posted in a public issue.

Commissions are independent research, not investment recommendations, legal counsel, or medical advice. Work may use AI-assisted research and drafting with human direction, verification, and editorial judgment. Confidentiality and permitted uses are agreed before work begins.
